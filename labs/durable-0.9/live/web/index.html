A link can be forwarded

Anyone with this link can view available location updates until sharing ends. Keep it only with people you trust. No app or account is needed to view this page.

This page is not emergency response. It does not dispatch help or confirm that the person is safe.

Activity can leave traces

Device activity, browsing, copied links and messages may be monitored or leave records. Removing a link from the address bar or clearing this page cannot erase browser, clipboard, message or device records.

Updates have limits

Location can be approximate, old, inaccurate or unavailable. The sender’s device, permission, battery, connection or Android controls can interrupt updates. An interrupted connection does not tell you why, or whether the person is safe.

What the service receives

The service operator processes the sender’s coordinates. HTTPS protects transport; this is not end-to-end encrypted. The server keeps the latest accepted point rather than a route history, but recipients can keep copies.

Last known and external maps

During connection loss this tab can retain its last received coordinates in memory, clearly marked unverified with their timestamp, age and accuracy. Paused updates are not a Stop. A reload cannot recover that tab-only point from storage. A confirmed Stop, chosen expiry or Clear removes it from this page; previously saved copies remain outside its control.

The optional Map action opens Google Maps only after disclosure and your explicit choice. Google receives the chosen last-known coordinates and device/network data under its own privacy policy. No viewing token, share identifier or generation is included in the map URL, and the action sends no page referrer. No map tiles, address lookup or geocoding load automatically.

What this page uses

This page does not request your location, embed maps or automatically load third-party content. It keeps the viewing credential in page memory, without local storage, session storage or cookies. Refreshing or reopening the cleaned address may require the original link.
