## Peer options and eligibility

- **RAINN Youth HelpRoom:** [provider page and ground rules](https://rainn.org/help-and-healing/hotline/youth-helproom/) describes moderated text rooms for ages 14–24, separated into 14–17 and 18–24. **Current session schedule/availability is unverified**: dates lack a clear year/timezone. The app labels this uncertainty and links to provider information. Moderators are mandatory reporters; identifying disclosures may trigger reporting to authorities. No unconditional anonymity/confidentiality claim.
- **After Silence:** current [registration notice](https://www.aftersilence.org/forum/index.php?/topic/151788-attention-new-survivors-registering-at-after-silence/) and [terms](https://www.aftersilence.org/forum/index.php?/topic/2390-terms-of-service/) require **18+**. Valid non-disposable email, validation, birth date and moderator approval required. Approval is not immediate. Public areas visible without membership; communications are not private. Old `join.php` has conflicting obsolete under-age instructions and is deliberately not used.
- **1in6:** [official online group page](https://1in6.org/support-group-chat) and [group terms PDF](https://1in6.org/1in6/documents/OSGs-terms-of-service.pdf): male-identifying survivors **18+**, free facilitated text groups, scheduled sessions and limited capacity. Not therapy/crisis care. Participant eligibility cannot be fully verified; copying/screenshotting remains possible. [JotCast privacy](https://www.jotcast.com/privacy.html) applies, including technical information/cookies. No claim of full anonymity/confidentiality.
- **NCMEC Team HOPE:** [operator information](https://www.missingkids.org/gethelpnow/support/teamhope) describes free phone matching with trained peer volunteers for families of missing/exploited children and eligible adult survivors of childhood exploitation. It is not a forum. Specific minimum family-support age, guaranteed hours and response time not established; app directs users to service eligibility. [NCMEC privacy policy](https://www.missingkids.org/footer/privacypolicy) applies, including specified information sharing.

No additional geographic restrictions for the online peer groups were established from these pages, so the app does not promise worldwide access. Capacity/current session participation was not tested. Links and policies require future maintenance; the app has no automated source refresh.

## Expanded resource finding and Android implementation sources

- [Official referral directory](https://humantraffickinghotline.org/en/service-provider-search): searchable by city, state or services; a selection of providers, not guaranteed openings/eligibility. Safe Bridge only opens it and sends no coordinates.
- [Android location permissions](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/location/permissions) and [runtime precision](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/location/permissions/runtime): optional foreground location, approximate access accepted; foreground service can retain foreground location while the screen is off.
- [Foreground service types](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/fgs/service-types) and [launch rules](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/fgs/launch): location type, prerequisite permissions, visible user start and prompt foreground notification.
- [Active apps Stop](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/fgs/handle-user-stopping): can terminate the whole process without callbacks; no continuity guarantee.
- [Android Sharesheet](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/sharing/send): ACTION_SEND/text/plain/chooser sends one snapshot to a user-chosen app, not live tracking or receipt confirmation.
- [Location reference](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/location/Location): fix elapsed time for age, accuracy is a horizontal estimate. [LocationManager](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/location/LocationManager) distinguishes current requests and cached last-known data.
- [Handler](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/os/Handler) and [AlarmManager](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/AlarmManager): deep sleep/inexact alarms can delay cleanup. An elapsed deadline is checked before using a session fix; exact physical service-stop timing is not promised.

These are implementation requirements reviewed against official docs, not evidence of Android runtime behavior in this environment. The 0.8 lab adds direct HTTPS access only to a separately configured live service; its default endpoint is blank. System location providers may process information under their own policies.

The recipient creates that URL only after separate disclosure/confirmation. No service availability or response precision was tested.

Opening a chosen coordinate externally is disclosed as a transfer to Google under its own retention/privacy terms. Only coordinates enter the map URL; no Safe Bridge session capability is included.
- No automatic map tiles or geocoding is implemented. Street address remains unavailable. [Public Nominatim usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/) cautions against submitting confidential/personal material; this project does not use that public geocoder for private positions.
